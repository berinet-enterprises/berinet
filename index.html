A little about us

Big ideas deserve
to be hands-on.

At Berinet Enterprises, we believe the best learning happens when learners can see it, hold it, and explore it for themselves. From vivid CBC wall charts to classroom-ready science and maths models, we help Kenyan educators turn the curriculum into something students can touch.

We're based in Lanet, Nakuru, and work with teachers, school leaders, bookshops and parents to find the right resources for every classroom.
